I am an undergraduate student at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, also enrolled at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research focuses on **ML Systems** -- specifically, how runtime infrastructure should be designed as workloads, hardware platforms, and system assumptions continue to evolve.

I am currently a research intern at [OrderLab](https://orderlab.io/), supervised by Prof. Ryan Huang, working on fault tolerance and adaptive execution for machine learning workloads. My recent projects include **PhoenixML**, a fine-grained fault recovery system for distributed GPU training, and **CudaProxy**, an adaptive execution runtime for ML inference.

I am applying for PhD programs in Fall 2026, with interests in building principled systems that remain resilient, adaptive, and efficient in the face of change.
